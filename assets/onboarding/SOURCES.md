Downloaded 2026-09-26, unmodified. These are the original transparent PNGs (400x400, RGBA), taken from the URLs in
the repo's own metadata (`content/3dicons-meta/<name>.md`, the `color:` field). Don't swap them for the site's
`.../storage/v1/object/public/sizes/...webp` previews: those are flattened onto white (no alpha channel) and show up
as white squares.
